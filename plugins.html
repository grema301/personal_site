KickDucker

Shipped

A sidechain-driven dynamic EQ. It runs real-time YIN pitch detection on a bass or 808 signal and ducks the kick drum at that signal's fundamental frequency — and its harmonics — instead of the whole low end, so the kick keeps its punch without fighting the bass for space.

  • Envelope-following attack and release
  • Harmonic attenuation at 2×, 3×, and 4× the fundamental
  • Note quantisation and frequency lock
  • Wet/dry mix
  • FFT spectrum visualiser
  • VST3
  • C++
  • JUCE

Preverb

In development

A reverse-reverb tail generator — the rising swoosh that leads into a sound, produced as a single plugin instead of the usual manual workaround of reversing audio, printing a reverb, and reversing it back.

Currently working through the lookahead buffer and latency design — sizing the buffer correctly before any of the reverb engine gets written.
