Plugins & downloads

Two VST3 plugins, free to download

Written in C++ with JUCE. Each exists because I wanted a tool that didn't. Downloads come straight from GitHub Releases.

KickDucker

Released

A sidechain-driven dynamic EQ. It runs real-time YIN pitch detection on a bass or 808 and ducks the kick at that note's fundamental and harmonics, instead of the whole low end, so the kick keeps its punch without fighting the bass.

  • Envelope-following attack and release
  • Ducks 2×, 3× and 4× harmonics
  • Note quantisation and frequency lock
  • FFT spectrum display

Preverb

Released

A reverse-reverb swell generator: the rising whoosh that leads into a vocal or a drum hit, made in one plugin instead of reversing, printing and re-reversing audio by hand. It detects the hit, builds the swell from the sound itself, and lands it exactly on the downbeat.

  • Live mode, or drop in an audio file
  • Tempo-synced swell, with a live view of capture and render
  • Drag the finished swell straight onto your timeline
  • Lookahead switch: bounce with it, then turn the delay off
